**Nvnest turns documents into verified data.** Upload invoices and business documents. Nvnest extracts the fields you need, shows you exactly where each value came from, and lets your team review before it moves into your workflow.

## What Nvnest does

- **Extracts the fields you need** — vendor names, invoice totals, due dates, line items, and other values from invoices, purchase orders, bills of lading, and packing slips.
- **Shows where each value came from** — every extracted field is linked to its exact page and position on the original document, so nothing has to be taken on faith.
- **Lets your team review before export** — uncertain or conflicting values are flagged and routed to a person for approval, rather than being silently guessed. Only verified data moves into your ERP, CRM, or accounting system.

## What this blog covers

This blog publishes practical, source-linked guides for finance, accounts payable (AP), and procurement teams:

- **Invoice and AP automation** — reducing manual data entry and invoice cycle time without adding headcount.
- **Document data extraction** — turning PDFs and scans into structured, ERP-ready records.
- **Verification and traceability** — linking extracted data back to its source for audit and accuracy.
- **Exception handling** — resolving mismatched invoices, purchase orders, and shipment details, conflicting dates, and low-confidence fields.

## How the content is written

Each guide focuses on a single question that finance and operations teams actually ask, answers it directly, and then explains the workflow in steps, tables, and checklists. Where relevant, guides cite external references such as IRS recordkeeping guidance, the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and ISO date/time standards.
